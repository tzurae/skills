Require every `to-tickets` issue to read as a self-contained causal chain for a fresh session.

Each issue now starts with a user-grounded Why that includes a concrete example and supporting evidence. What must be an ordered solution path whose steps name the pain they remove and the observation that proves them effective. How must move from system flow into implementation details that enable those steps. Falsifiable success metrics cover the user outcome, behaviour contract, and relevant regression gates.

The review now happens in two passes: approve the ticket breakdown first, then review complete issue bodies one at a time unless the user requests a batch. Both local and real-tracker templates carry the same Why, What, How, success-metrics, and blocking-edge structure.
